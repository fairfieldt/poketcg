# Ronald iteration 1

Reference: `concepts/ronald.png`. Reused the approved Mark body's joined
shoulders, rigid pivots and shoe construction. Ronald has a new competitive
face and one owning closed head/scalp/ear/nose shell with six broad crown points;
there is no cap, backpack or separate spike mesh.

Actual native capture completed 16 frames. Raw and packed audit passes at
512 triangles, parts 0–4 and one opaque 32×32 face texture. The owning head has
180 edges, all incident to two faces; hair forms one connected component.
Editable Blender and mesh/UV data are retained.

Viewed native portrait, room, conversation and four-pose turnaround. Blue
sleeves remain joined and the eyes/smirk differ from Mark. The head has profile
depth and tan ears. Hair is too vertically pointed and narrow compared with
the concept: the top reads as a crown instead of broad swept-back wedges.
The next paragraph records the independent review.

Independent review agreed: lower peaks 0.10–0.14, broaden/sweep the scalp
rearward, increase eye whites slightly and separate the brows, and strengthen
the smirk. Those observations drove iteration 2.

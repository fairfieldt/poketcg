Final candidate: `i3`. The broader mature jaw, swept white crown, monocle and dark moustache distinguish Jack from the earlier generic clean-shaven model. White jacket sleeves join the torso through walking poses. The monocle and moustache are simplified face painting, not loose geometry.

All rounds retain the L643 export, opaque 32×32 PNGs, editable `jack.blend`,
`jack/authoring.json`, `source.py`, recipe snapshot, geometry/topology audits,
and eight actual native before/candidate diagnostic frames. The baseline is
this family's real production export. Only the review target changes.

Final owning head is closed and consistently wound; scalp/hood is connected.
Five rigid parts preserve the walk rig. No triangles were filtered to conceal
defects. Fine detail is simplified at native resolution. Production assets have
not been replaced.

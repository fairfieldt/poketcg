# Round 1 — native review

Reviewed `native/sheet.png` and its seven actual 320×240 framebuffer captures from the production N64 renderer in ares with 4 MiB. The Blender preview is separate and is not capture evidence.

Both cats are distinguishable in the conversation views: the tuxedo has a clear white bib and paws; the tabby has a dark forehead and body stripes. Painted eyes read more cleanly than the baseline's protruding eye spheres. The portrait shows a complete rounded skull and the four-angle turnaround confirms profile/rear volume. At the room camera, the cats remain small and the facial features are not individually readable.

The ears are overly tall and narrow, especially in profile. The tabby's forehead M becomes a dense zigzag and its side/rear head has large rectangular dark patches. The pale tuxedo muzzle is broader than the photo's mostly grey upper muzzle and white chin. The profile still needs a more deliberate muzzle projection.

Next round: shorten and broaden ears; simplify the forehead M; replace rectangular head patches with narrow cheek stripes; deepen the owning muzzle surface; restrict the tuxedo white to the lower muzzle/chin. Preserve the compact body and leg proportions. Both exports pass independent raw/packed audits at 728 triangles, five rigid parts, two opaque 32×32 textures, and a closed consistently wound skull.

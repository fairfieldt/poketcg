# Approved adventure image, iteration 2

Reviewed iteration 1 native frames with an independent reviewer. Revised head
width +13%, depth +10%, shoulder width -5%, softer jaw taper, wider irises and
level outer eyebrows. The crown rim moved down slightly without height growth.
Retained native capture has 16 completed frames. Raw and packed audits pass:
584 triangles, five rigid parts, three 32×32 textures.

Native review: head/shoulder proportions are closer to the reference. Cap seam
is clean. However the side of the hair reads as a rectangular panel and the
ribbon fringe remains visibly separate at its roots. Side forehead and cheek
remain too flat. The user rejected the disconnected-looking black sleeves and
vest details, and explicitly requested one plain red shirt.

Iteration 3 decisions from this review: eliminate the fringe ribbon entirely.
Make three broad locks the varying lower boundary of the owning scalp shell;
keep their roots continuous under the cap rim. Use a solid red torso and upper
sleeves with deeper shoulder overlap. Remove the front flattening clamp and
increase real cranial/cheek depth, retaining front-plane UV mapping only.

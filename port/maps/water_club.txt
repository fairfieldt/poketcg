WATER CLUB - 3D room brief
==========================
Image: water_club.png - 28 x 32 tiles = 224 x 256 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: WATER_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first (coordinates,
scale, camera).  This is the only club room with a diagonal plan: it is an
indoor pool / conservatory, not a hall.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y30 -> WATER_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.  Only
  warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... the deck only: x2-25, y2-28 minus the pool, the
                        planted bed and the props.  The pool and the bed are
                        impassable.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y28-31.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27.
  Wall build: white #ffffff face, a light-cyan #00adff pinstripe, and a
  full-width cyan/white 8 px checkerboard frieze along y2 (and y30), with the
  same checkerboard on the side wall bands.  Black outline at the outer edge.
  This is the brightest, cleanest wall in the game - an aquatics-centre
  livery.  Height 4-5 units.

FLOOR
  Deck tile everywhere: pale yellow #ffffa5 squares with thick orange #ff8400
  grout, laid as 2 x 2-tile (16 px) blocks (four tile variants make one
  block).  Flat, one texture.  It reads as painted pool-side tiling.

THE POOL - the room's subject, and it is NOT rectangular
  A sunken swimming pool of deep blue #1800ff water, occupying the middle of
  the room.  Its outline, given row by row so you can rebuild it exactly
  (inclusive x ranges of open water):
      y3  x11-17      y8  x6-17
      y4  x10-17      y9  x5-17
      y5  x9-17       y10 x4-17
      y6  x8-17       y11 x4-13
      y7  x7-17       y12..y16  x4-13
  So: a rectangle from y11 to y16 with its east edge at x13; above y10 it
  widens to x17; and its north-west corner is cut by a clean 45-degree
  chamfer running from (11,3) down to (4,10).
  Coping, from the water outward: a dark navy #000042 waterline, then a
  light-cyan #00adff band, then a white #ffffff band, then the deck.  The
  coping is a real raised lip - model it 0.1-0.2 units proud of the deck with
  a rounded top, and it mitres at the 45-degree corner.
  Water surface: flat deep blue with a few 2 x 1-tile white/cyan ripple
  highlights scattered at random, plus a couple of single white sparkles.
  Give the water a flat unlit blue with 2-3 static ripple decals; no
  animation is needed to match the original.
  FISH: two, 2 x 2 tiles each, drawn as blue fish with white bellies and dark
  outlines - one at x16-17, y5-6 (head to the right) and one at x4-5, y12-13
  (head to the left).  They are the only living things drawn in the pool.

PLANTED BED - the north-west corner
  A triangular raised bed of moss and shrubs that fills the corner behind the
  pool's diagonal: bright green #00ff00 ground with mid-green #21ad08 and
  dark-green #085200 fern/shrub clumps.  Its hypotenuse is parallel to the
  pool's chamfer, 1-2 tiles north-west of it, running from about (11,3) down
  to (2,9); its other edges are the north and west walls.
  Two or three tall palms stand in it at x4-7, y1-2 - their fronds are drawn
  ABOVE the wall band, i.e. they are taller than the wall.  Treat them as
  4-5 units tall, the tallest objects in the room.
  The bed is impassable and about 0.3 units high (a low kerbed planter).

CHAISE LOUNGES
  White slatted horizontal seats with raised backs at one end, navy frames
  and orange feet/arm ends. The south-east source crop contains two lounges,
  one north of the other. Keep Amy's supported seat height at 0.71 units.
  Source crops: x20-24/y4-6, x5-9/y20-22, x22-25/y22-26.

PARASOL TABLES - three
  The red-and-cream striped radial canopies stand above cream round tables
  outlined in navy, with orange poles and dark legs. They are poolside
  furniture, not carousel horses. Build a low-poly striped parasol canopy,
  thin support and a small round tabletop with a navy/orange base.
  Positions: x20-23/y2-3, x4-7/y18-20, x24-25/y14-16.
  Keep each assembly within its original blocked floor footprint.

PLOTTED PLANTS
  * Four potted shrubs in a tight 2 x 2 block at x14-17, y11-12, on the deck
    in the notch east of the pool: orange pots, round green bushes.
  * One potted palm in the south-west corner at x2-3, y24-27: a fat brown
    #c66b00 pot, a thick trunk and a fan of green fronds, about 2 units tall.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_AMY x22,y4 and NPC_JOSHUA x22,y8 on the east deck, NPC_SARA x6,y18
  west, NPC_AMANDA x22,y20 south-east.  Keep the deck ring around the pool
  clear - it is 2-4 tiles wide everywhere and is the only walkable surface.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Yellow deck, deep blue water, cyan-and-white walls, green plants, and only
  the red-and-cream parasols as a warm accent.  High key, very saturated.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE WATER CLUB
  The big chamfered indoor pool with a raised cyan-and-white coping and two
  fish in it, the triangular planted bed with palms growing over the wall, and
  lounges and parasol tables arranged around the water on yellow tiling.
  Nothing is symmetric: the whole room is organised around one diagonal.

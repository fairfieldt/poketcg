HALL OF HONOR - 3D room brief
=============================
Image: hall_of_honor.png - 24 x 26 tiles = 192 x 208 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: HALL_OF_HONOR.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  The
sanctum at the top of the Pokemon Dome, where the five Legendary Cards are
kept.  A small, perfectly symmetric shrine room - the most formal interior in
the game.

CONNECTION
  South door x10-13 at y24 -> POKEMON_DOME at x14,y2 / x16,y2.
  Only warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-21, y5-21 minus the shrine, pillars, torches and
                        medallions.  The shrine block is not walkable.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y22-25.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x22-23.
  Wall build: dark olive-brown #211008 panelling with a gold #bd6300 stripe
  and a white outline; the decorated row (y1, mirrored at y23) is a brown-and-
  cream 8 px checkerboard on a gold rail.  Height 4-5 units.  Same wall
  family as the dome - this is the same building.

FLOOR
  A gold-and-white checkerboard: 2 x 2-tile squares of bright yellow #ffe700
  alternating with white #ffffff, each yellow square shaded with a diagonal
  orange #ffa531 hatch and each white square with a pale yellow cross-hatch,
  all on a 4-tile pitch with red #ff0000 grout lines.  Flat decal, the whole
  room.  It should read as polished brass and marble.

ENTRANCE RUNNER
  x10-13, y23-25: a red #ff0000 carpet with white arrow/chevron marks laid
  across the threshold, 4 tiles wide, running straight at the shrine.

CORNER MEDALLIONS - four, mirrored
  2 x 2-tile square plaques set into the floor at the four corners of the
  room: x2-3,y3-4; x20-21,y3-4; x2-3,y20-21; x20-21,y20-21.  Each is a
  white field with a dark-blue #000052 interlaced knotwork pattern inside a
  blue #7b84ff border.  Flat inlays, impassable, about 1 unit across.

THE SHRINE - the focus, dead centre
  x7-16, y9-14: a stepped blue-and-white altar block, symmetric about x=11.5.
    * y9:      the upper step, x8-15.
    * y10-14:  the main block, x7-16.
  Build it as two stacked platforms with white faces, blue #7b84ff fluting and
  a dark #000052 base - about 1.2 and 1.8 units high.  On its top sits a
  dark blue POKE BALL crest, x11-14, y10-12. In the 3D room this is a shallow
  relief on the shrine's back panel, leaving the floating cards prominent.
  Down the front face, y12-14, run
  horizontal blue-and-white banded steps.

PILLARS - two, mirrored
  x7-8, y10-14 and x15-16, y10-14: two white columns with blue fluting and
  a notched capital, standing at the front corners of the shrine and rising
  above it to about 3 units.

TORCHES - two, mirrored
  x8, y8 and x14, y8: slender posts behind the shrine topped by a red
  #ef3100 and orange flame head on a white stem, about 2.5 units tall.

LEGENDARY AUTO DECK MACHINE - north wall, centre
  x10-13, y1-4: the separate blue-and-white deck-building console. The map
  objects at (10,2) and (12,2) call Script_fbe1, whose Text05b5-05b7 names it
  the Legendary Auto Deck Machine and opens DECK_MACHINE_LEGENDARY.
  Give it a cream frame, blue display, card slot and one red activation key.
  The central shrine instead calls Script_fbf1 and awards the Legendary Cards.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG) - the Legendary Cards
  Four card components and two sparks float around the shrine:
  NPC_LEGENDARY_CARD_TOP_LEFT x10,y8, NPC_LEGENDARY_CARD_TOP_RIGHT
  x12,y8, NPC_LEGENDARY_CARD_LEFT_SPARK x8,y10, NPC_LEGENDARY_CARD_BOTTOM_
  LEFT x10,y10, NPC_LEGENDARY_CARD_BOTTOM_RIGHT x12,y10, NPC_LEGENDARY_
  CARD_RIGHT_SPARK x14,y10. The four card actors are graphical quadrants:
  AnimFrameTable18 assembles one cream-bordered purple card with a pulsing
  pink circular aura. The two sparks blink beside that composite icon.
  Keep the runner axis x10-13 clear from the door to the shrine.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Brass-and-marble checker floor, olive-and-gold walls, one blue-and-white
  shrine with a dark Poké Ball crest, two torches, one deck machine, one red runner.
  Very few hues, high contrast, ceremonial.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE HALL OF HONOR
  A stepped blue-and-white altar with a dark Poké Ball crest, flanked by
  two fluted pillars and two red-flamed torches, with a separate deck machine
  against the north wall; a gold-and-white floor with four blue knotwork medallions
  in the corners and a red arrowed runner laid from the door straight to the
  altar.

ROCK CLUB - 3D room brief
=========================
Image: rock_club.png - 28 x 30 tiles = 224 x 240 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: ROCK_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first (coordinates,
scale, camera).  This is the archetypal "club room": perfectly symmetric about
the room's vertical axis, one long central axis, mirrored rows of props.
Theme: a cave/canyon shrine.  Blue stone floor, gold statues, brown boulders.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y28 -> ROCK_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.  Only
  warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north from the door, straight down the
  carpet - the composition is a single one-point perspective corridor.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-25, y4-25 minus the props, plus the carpet aisle
                        x12-15 which is walkable end to end from y2 to y28.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y26-29.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27.
  Wall build, three rows, repeated on the north and south bands:
    row 0 (y0 / y26) - black outline over a violet #5242ce face with a salmon
                       #ff845a pinstripe.
    row 1 (y1 / y27) - a white #ffffff band carrying a salmon #ff845a
                       zigzag/diamond frieze, broken every 2 tiles by a small
                       violet #5242ce square set into the frieze (12 of them
                       per wall).  This reads as a carved strata band.
    row 2 (y2 / y28) - a violet balustrade: a row of dark vertical bars with
                       white caps, running the full width.
  The side wall bands (x0-1, x26-27) are the violet face with a vertical
  salmon stripe - the same material, seen from above.
  Wall height: build 4-5 units.

FLOOR - two materials, laid in a strict plan
  1. BLUE STONE TILE (the main floor): light blue #8cceff squares with a hard
     blue #0031de border and white grout highlights, one tile per square.
     Laid as rectangular "pools" that fill everything between the wall band
     and the rock work.  Flat, unlit, one texture.
  2. GRAVEL + ROCKS (the border): a white #ffffff gravel bed with #efa518
     orange speckles, with brown boulders sitting on it.  Wherever the gravel
     appears it replaces the blue tile, so the room reads as: blue tile floor
     edged and interrupted by gravel and boulders.

CENTRAL AXIS - the room's spine
  * RED CARPET: x12-15, y3-28.  4 tiles wide, running the entire length of
    the room from the north wall out through the south doorway.  Built as:
      x12 and x15 - solid gold #ffce18 borders with a dotted edge;
      x13 and x14 - red #ff1000 field with a white #ffffff diamond lattice
                    (a running argyle of small diamonds and cross dots) and a
                    dark crimson #631000 edge line.
    At y3 the carpet has a distinct finished end panel (a fringe/head band)
    against the north wall.  Lay it flat on the floor, 1-2 cm proud.
  * Two GRAVEL-AND-ROCK AISLES flank the carpet: x9-11 and x16-18, each 3
    tiles wide, running the full height y3-25.  They are continuous gravel
    beds scattered with loose rocks and they carry the statues.

STATUES - six, in a perfect 2 x 3 grid (the room's identity)
  Positions (2 tiles wide x 4 tall each): x10-11 and x16-17, at y3-6, y10-13
  and y18-21.  Three per side, evenly spaced 7 tiles apart, standing in the
  gravel aisles and facing the carpet.
  Construction: 2 x 3 of carved figure on top of a 2 x 1 pedestal block.
  The figure is a stylised totem - a rounded head/canopy over a narrow waist
  and flared base, in gold #ffce18 with #a56b00 mid-tone and black outlines.
  The pedestal is a gold frame with a dark inset panel on its face.
  Total height about 2 units.  Model one statue and instance it six times.

BOULDERS - the perimeter rock work, all mirrored left/right
  * FOUR CORNER PILES, 4 x 4 tiles each: x2-5 y3-6, x22-25 y3-6,
    x2-5 y22-25, x22-25 y22-25.  Big rounded boulders in brown #efa518 /
    #842900 with heavy black outlines and white speculars, on gravel.
    About 1.5 units tall, impassable.
  * TWO SIDE FORMATIONS, 4 x 7 tiles each: x2-5 y10-16 and x22-25 y10-16,
    at the mid-point of the side walls, directly opposite each other.  Taller,
    multi-boulder stacks; the largest impassable objects in the room.
  * EIGHT SMALL ROCK CLUSTERS scattered against the corner and side piles:
    2 x 2 at x4-5 y10-11, x22-23 y10-11, x4-5 y15-16, x22-23 y15-16;
    2 x 1 at x2-3 y13 and x24-25 y13; single rocks at x4 y5, x23 y5,
    x6 y13, x21 y13, x4 y23, x23 y23.
  * Loose rocks and gravel patches are scattered continuously along the two
    aisles x9-11 and x16-18 between the statues.
  Total impassable rock work: 6 large piles + 8 small clusters + the aisle
  scatter.  Nothing sits on the blue tile: the boulders always sit on gravel.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_GENE x12,y6 - standing on the carpet in the north third, between the
  first pair of statues (this is the club's opponent).
  NPC_RYAN x20,y14 and NPC_ANDREW x6,y20 - on the east and west blue-tile
  pools.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Cool blue floor, hot gold/brown rock and statuary, one saturated red
  carpet down the middle, violet-and-salmon walls.  Keep the blue flat and the
  rock work hard-outlined; no gradients, no bump maps.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE ROCK CLUB
  The red carpet corridor flanked by two gravel aisles holding six gold
  totem-statues in a 2 x 3 grid, with brown boulder piles in the four corners
  and at the middle of both side walls, all on a blue-tile floor.  If you
  build the carpet, six statues and six boulder piles, the room is done.

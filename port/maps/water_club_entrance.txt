WATER CLUB ENTRANCE - 3D room brief
===================
Image: water_club_entrance.png - 20 x 18 tiles = 160 x 144 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: WATER_CLUB_ENTRANCE - the Water club's entrance foyer.
Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first: coordinate system, scale, and the
camera convention (the top band of the image is the north wall in elevation).

THIS ROOM IS A COPY - BUILD THE SHELL ONCE
  All nine club foyers share one tilemap and one tileset.  Comparing this map
  with fighting_club_entrance.png gives 354 identical tiles out of 360.
  The ONLY difference in the entire room is the 4 x 4 emblem plaque in the
  middle of the floor (16 tiles, plus its own palette).  Everything below is
  the shared shell; the emblem section at the end is the only club-specific
  part.  Do not retheme the walls or the floor: the foyer is deliberately the
  same corporate livery in front of every club.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ............. x2-17, y3-13 (16 x 11 tiles) plus the door
                               passages below.
  North wall band ............ y0-2, drawn in elevation.
  South wall band ............ y14-17: the same decoration mirrored, plus one
                               black edge row at y17 that is not geometry.
  West wall band ............. x0-1.        East wall band .... x18-19.
  Wall build: dark brown #5a4229 face, gold #c6ad31 frame moulding turning
  the corners, white #ffffff inner field, black outline.  Identical livery to
  the club lobbies.
  Doors (each 4 tiles wide, cut flush through the wall band, no doors - just
  openings):
    north door x8-11 at y0 ... -> WATER_CLUB (the club room).  A red runner
                                  rug fills the opening and runs 1 tile into
                                  the room: x8-11, y0-3.
    south door x8-11 at y17 .. -> the street (OVERWORLD_MAP).  No rug.
    west door  x0 at y6-9 .... -> WATER_CLUB_LOBBY.  A red rug with a
                                  maroon border and pale diamonds at x0-2,
                                  y7-10.
  So the foyer is a hub: street to the south, lobby to the west, club to the
  north.  Camera: fixed, looking north from just inside the south door.

FLOOR
  One material: pale yellow-green #deffb5 with bright green #00ff31 grout on
  the east and south edge of each tile, reading as 2 x 2-tile pale squares
  with bright green joints.  Flat, unlit, one texture.

WALL DECORATION (the north band's decoration is mirrored exactly on the south
band)
  * Wainscot panels: gold/white 8 px checkerboard, x2-6 and x13-17 at y1-2,
    and the same two at y15-16.  5 x 2 tiles each, four total.
  * Two framed pictures per wall: x3-4 and x15-16 at y1-2 (and the same x at
    y15-16).  2 x 2 tiles: dark brown frame, white mat, a small green-and-
    orange abstract picture.  Hung at eye height, 3 units apart.
  * Gold vertical mouldings at x7 and x12 (y1-2 and y15-16) trim the north
    and south door openings.

PROPS
  * One potted palm, south-east corner: x16-17, y10-13 (2 x 4 tiles).
    Round orange #c66b00 pot with a dark body and a fan of green #21ad08
    fronds; about 1.5 units tall.  The only prop standing on the floor.

  * CLUB EMBLEM PLAQUE - the one unique piece.  x8-11, y6-9 (4 x 4 tiles),
    set flush into the floor in the exact centre of the room, on the
    north-south axis between the two doors.
    From the outside in:
      1. a 1-tile black outline all round;
      2. a flat field in blue `#0073ff`;
      3. a small hollow square in each of the four corners;
      4. a stepped ring/annulus in white `#ffffff` filling the middle;
      5. on the field, inside the ring: a fish curving clockwise with its head at the left, in cyan `#00fff7` with near-black `#001029` on the back and tail and a dark eye.
    Palette: `#ffffff` white, `#00fff7` cyan, `#0073ff` blue, `#001029` near-black.
    Build it as a 4 x 4 decal 1-2 cm above the floor, or as an inlaid
    medallion.  It has to read from the south door - it is the whole point of
    the room.

NPC
  NPC_CLERK1 is scripted at x6, y2 - on top of the north wall band, between
  the wainscot panel and the north doorway.  He is the club greeter.  Put his
  standing position just south of the wall (x6, y3) so he does not clip.

LIGHTING / MOOD
  Flat shading, no coloured light.  The emblem is the only saturated object in
  the room; keep the livery colours so the badge reads from the street door.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM WATER
  Only the emblem plaque at x8-11, y6-9.  Everything else in this file is
  identical to the other eight club foyers.

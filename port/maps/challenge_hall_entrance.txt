CHALLENGE HALL ENTRANCE - 3D room brief
=======================================
Image: challenge_hall_entrance.png - 20 x 18 tiles = 160 x 144 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: CHALLENGE_HALL_ENTRANCE - the foyer of the Challenge Hall, the room
where you sign up for the Challenge Cup.
Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first (coordinates, scale, camera).

THIS IS THE SHARED CLUB FOYER WITH THE EMBLEM DELETED
  Comparing this map with fighting_club_entrance.png gives 344 identical tiles
  out of 360, and all 16 differing tiles are in one place: x8-11, y6-9, where
  the other clubs have their emblem plaque.  Here the floor is plain.  There
  is no other change anywhere - same walls, same doors, same rug, same palm,
  same framed pictures, same colours.  Build the shared foyer shell (see any
  *_club_entrance.txt, they are all the same) and leave the centre empty.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ............. x2-17, y3-13 plus the door passages.
  North wall band y0-2 (elevation) / south wall band y14-17 / west x0-1 /
  east x18-19.  Dark brown #5a4229 face, gold #c6ad31 moulding, white inner
  field, black outline.
  Doors: north x8-11 at y0 -> CHALLENGE_HALL, with a red runner x8-11 y0-3;
         south x8-11 at y17 -> the street;
         west x0 at y6-9 -> CHALLENGE_HALL_LOBBY, with a red rug x0-2 y7-10.
  Wall decoration: gold/white checkerboard wainscot x2-6 and x13-17 at y1-2
  and y15-16; two 2 x 2 framed pictures per wall at x3-4 and x15-16; gold
  door jamb mouldings at x7 and x12.
  Floor: pale yellow-green #deffb5 tiles with bright green #00ff31 grout.
  Prop: one potted palm at x16-17, y10-13.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE CHALLENGE HALL
  Nothing in the art.  It is the generic foyer with no club badge - which is
  correct, because the Challenge Hall belongs to no element.  If you want a
  distinguishing cue, this is the room to hang a tournament banner in, but the
  original has none.

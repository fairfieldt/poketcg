POKEMON DOME - 3D room brief
============================
Image: pokemon_dome.png - 32 x 32 tiles = 256 x 256 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: POKEMON_DOME.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  The final
arena of the game and the largest room in it: a raised violet duel floor
floating in a red-and-gold carpet, with the duel platform dead centre.
Perfectly symmetric about x=15.5.

CONNECTION
  South door x14-17 at y30 -> POKEMON_DOME_ENTRANCE at x22,y2 / x24,y2.
  North door x14-17 at y0  -> HALL_OF_HONOR at x10,y22 / x12,y22.
  The room is a north-south axis; both doors are on the same centre line.
  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... the whole interior x2-29, y3-27 EXCEPT the raised
                        field edge (see RAISED FIELD), the platform, the
                        hedges and the two statues.
  North wall band ..... y0-2 and y7.   South wall band ..... y28-31.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x30-31.
  Wall build: dark olive-brown #5a4b00 panelling with a gold #c66b00 stripe
  and a white outline, and a brown-and-cream 8 px checkerboard on a gold rail
  along y1 and y29.  Height 4-5 units.

NORTH EMBLEMS - two
  x14-15 and x16-17, y1-2: two identical black-framed, gold-backed plaques,
  each carrying a red-and-white POKE BALL on a blue field.  They sit either
  side of the way through to the Hall of Honor.  Flat sign planes about
  1 x 1 units, 0.1 off the wall.

FLOOR - the carpet
  A red #ff0000 carpet with a gold #ffe784 figure, in two different weaves:
    * NORTH HALF (y3-6): bold gold diagonal stripes on red, running at 45
      degrees on a 1-tile pitch - the upper concourse.
    * SOUTH HALF (y21-27): a gold octagon-and-cross lattice with dark crimson
      #a50000 dots on red - the lower concourse.
  Both are flat decals.  Between them, at y7, runs a white-and-gold skirting
  that marks the foot of the raised field.

RAISED FIELD - the arena
  A raised platform of violet-blue #6b52ff tiles with a darker #1818a5 grid
  on every tile edge, outlined along its whole perimeter with a bright yellow
  #ffff00 curb 1 tile wide.  Its outline:
      y8        x4-13 and x18-27          (a 4-tile notch at x14-17 for the
                                           north way through)
      y9-y11    x4-27
      y12-y18   x4-13 and x18-27          (the platform occupies the notch)
      y19-y20   x4-27
      y21       x5-26
      y22       x6-13 and x18-26
      y23       x7-13 and x18-26
  Raise it about 0.5 units above the carpet and give it a plain violet
  riser.  Walkable on top; the curb is the only visual edge.
  Two ramps step it down to the carpet at the south corners: a wedge of dark
  blue treads with yellow nosing descending from (4,18) to (9,22) on the west
  and mirrored from (27,18) to (22,22) on the east.

POKE BALL FLOOR MOTIFS - two, mirrored
  Painted directly on the raised field at x8-11, y12-15 and x21-24, y12-15:
  four-tile-diameter POKE BALLS drawn as a red upper half, a yellow lower
  half, a dark band and a blue-and-white centre button.  Flat decals about
  2 units across; they are the arena's identity.

DUEL PLATFORM - dead centre
  x14-17, y12-17 (4 x 6 tiles) standing on the raised field: a bright green
  #21ad08 card field framed in brown #734200, marked with dark rectangular
  card bays and small white position markers, on a dark brown base with a
  vented toe at y18.  This is the actual duelling board - model it as a
  slightly raised dais, 0.3 units, with a flat green playing surface.

DUEL STATUES - two, mirrored
  Small red-and-blue serpent/dragon figures at x12-13, y14-15 and x18-19,
  y14-15, crouched either side of the duel platform facing outward.  About
  0.8 units tall, impassable.

HEDGES - two runs
  Flowered hedges fill the side channels between the field and the walls:
  x1-3, y9-20 on the west and x28-30, y9-20 on the east.  Green #21ad08 and
  dark-green #085200 foliage thickly dotted with red #ff0000 flowers, with
  brown #c66b00 trunks showing at intervals.  About 1 unit tall, impassable.
  They run the full height of the field and hide its edges.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  The Challenge Cup finalists line the top of the field: NPC_JACK x8,y2,
  NPC_ROD x12,y2, NPC_COURTNEY x18,y2, NPC_STEVE x22,y2 - all on the upper
  concourse, evenly spaced either side of the north way through.
  NPC_RONALD1 x30,y0 at the top of the east hedge.  Leave the platform and
  the axis x14-17 clear.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Red and gold carpet, a violet field with a yellow curb, two giant Poké
  Balls on it, one green duel board at its heart, green-and-red hedges down
  the sides, and olive-and-gold walls.  Saturated, ceremonial, final.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE POKEMON DOME
  A raised violet duel floor with a yellow curb, painted with two giant
  Poké Balls, carrying a green duel platform guarded by two red serpent
  statues; red-and-gold striped carpet above it and lattice carpet below;
  flowered hedges in the side channels; twin Poké Ball plaques over the north
  door to the Hall of Honor.

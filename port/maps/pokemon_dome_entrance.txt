POKEMON DOME ENTRANCE - 3D room brief
=====================================
Image: pokemon_dome_entrance.png - 32 x 18 tiles = 256 x 144 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: POKEMON_DOME_ENTRANCE.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.
A wide, low, richly carpeted vestibule: the ticket hall at the foot of the
Pokemon Dome.  It is a corridor, not a hall - you enter from the south and
leave north into the dome.

CONNECTION
  South door x14-17 at y16 -> OVERWORLD_MAP.
  North door x22 at y0 -> POKEMON_DOME at x14,y28;  x24 at y0 -> x16,y28.
  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-29, y3-14 minus the counters and machine below.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y14-16.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x30-31.
  Wall build: dark olive-brown #5a4b00 panelling with a gold #c66b00 stripe
  and a white #ffffff outline; the decorated row (y1-2, mirrored at y15) is a
  brown-and-cream 8 px checkerboard on a gold rail.  Height only about 3.5
  units - the room is deliberately low and wide.

FLOOR
  A heavy red #ff0000 carpet with a gold #ffe784 lattice: gold diamonds
  on a 2-tile pitch, each enclosing a dark crimson #a50000 dot, joined by
  gold crosses.  Flat, one texture, the whole room.  It is the same carpet
  family as the dome interior - this room is the outside of the same building.

TICKET KIOSKS - six, one mesh, two runs
  Each is a 2 x 4-tile counter stall about 1.6 units tall: an orange #c66b00
  carcass with a dark #291800 interior, one open shelf holding pale bottles
  and cups, and a closed orange cupboard with a dark knob below; a plain tan
  #c68c4a canopy panel caps it at y1.  Impassable.
    NORTH RUN - three at x2-3, x4-5, x6-7, y1-4, against the north wall.
    WEST RUN  - three at x2-3, x4-5, x6-7, y8-10, standing free in the room.
  Model one, instance six.

TICKET MACHINE - east wall
  x29-30, y1-4.  A blue #2939ff turnstile/turnstile-console: a white bezel,
  a blue screen, a blue key panel and a brown base.  About 1.5 units tall.

COUNTERS - north wall, east
  x22-23 and x24-25, y1-2: two low 2 x 2-tile counter stalls of the same
  orange construction, set into the wall either side of the way through.

"B1F" SIGN - north wall, centre
  x17-20, y1-2.  A black-framed, white-faced sign with a yellow #ffce00
  inset, carrying the letters B1F at the left and a yellow arrow/legend
  panel to the right.  A flat sign plane 0.1 units off the wall, about
  2 x 1 units.  It is the only lettering in the room.

POTTED PALM - south-east
  x28-29, y11-14.  A brown pot, a curved trunk and green #21ad08 fronds,
  about 1.8 units tall, standing just inside the south-east corner.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  None.  This is a pass-through; keep the axis x14-25 clear from the south
  door to the north opening.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Red and gold carpet over the whole floor, dark olive walls with a gold
  checker, orange kiosks, one blue machine, one yellow sign.  The palette is
  shared with the dome interior - build it as one material set.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE POKEMON DOME ENTRANCE
  A low, wide ticket vestibule carpeted head to foot in red-and-gold lattice,
  with six orange kiosk stalls in two runs of three, a blue ticket machine and
  a yellow "B1F" sign on the north wall, and an opening in the north-east
  corner that leads into the dome.

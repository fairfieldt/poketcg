EVENT - POKEMON DOME ENTRANCE, NORTH DOOR OPEN
==============================================
Image: events/pokemon_dome_entrance_door_open.png
This is NOT a separate room.  It is pokemon_dome_entrance.txt with the north
opening uncovered.  Build the base room once and swap this patch.

WHAT IS IDENTICAL
  Every wall, the red-and-gold carpet, all six ticket kiosks, the blue ticket
  machine, the "B1F" sign, the palm, the south door and the whole palette are
  unchanged, tile for tile.

WHAT CHANGES - exactly one 4 x 3-tile patch on the NORTH WALL
  x22-25, y0-2.
  In the BASE room this patch is closed: two low orange counter stalls sit at
  x22-23 and x24-25 on y1-2, backed by the olive wall and the brown-and-cream
  checker band, so the north wall reads as continuous and the way through is
  hidden.
  In THIS state the stalls and the whole wall band are gone.  The red-and-gold
  lattice carpet simply runs off the top edge at x22-25, bounded by the plain
  wall returns at x21 and x26.  It is an open doorway, not a door object:
  there is no leaf, no frame and no leaf swing - the wall is cut away and the
  floor continues through.

HOW TO BUILD THE OPENING
  Model the north wall as one mesh with a 4-tile-wide, 3-tile-tall opening at
  x22-25, plus a separate "closed" plug - the two counter stalls and the wall
  band segment - that is shown in the base state and hidden in this one.
  Give the opening a 0.1-unit black reveal and a thin gold #c66b00 jamb so it
  reads as a way through rather than a hole in the map.  It is the warp to
  POKEMON_DOME at x22 -> dome x14,y28 and x24 -> dome x16,y28, so keep it
  walkable and unobstructed in the open state.

VISUAL CUE
  The checker band on the north wall stops dead at x21 and restarts at x26 in
  the open state, so the opening is legible even from the south door.  Do not
  carry the checker over the gap.

EVENT - POKEMON DOME, HALL OF HONOR DOOR OPEN
=============================================
Image: events/pokemon_dome_hall_of_honor_door_open.png
This is NOT a separate room.  It is pokemon_dome.txt with the north doorway
uncovered.  Build the base room once and swap this patch.

WHAT IS IDENTICAL
  The raised violet field with its yellow curb, the two giant floor Poké
  Balls, the green duel platform, the two serpent statues, the ramps, both
  flowered hedges, the striped and lattice red carpets, the olive-and-gold
  walls, the south door and the whole palette are unchanged, tile for tile.

WHAT CHANGES - exactly one 4 x 3-tile patch on the NORTH WALL
  x14-17, y0-2.
  In the BASE room this patch carries the two POKE BALL PLAQUES: black-
  framed, gold-backed panels at x14-15 and x16-17, each showing a red-and-
  white Poké Ball on a blue field.  They sit side by side and seal the
  opening, so the north wall reads as a pair of ceremonial emblems.
  In THIS state the plaques are gone.  The red carpet with its gold diagonal
  stripes runs straight through the gap and off the top edge at x14-17,
  bounded by white-and-tan #c66b00 doorjambs standing at x13 and x18 on
  y1-2.  Again there is no door leaf - the emblem panels are the closed door;
  removing them opens the way.

HOW TO BUILD THE OPENING
  Model the north wall with a 4-tile-wide, 3-tile-tall opening at x14-17 and
  a separate "sealed" plug made of the two Poké Ball plaques plus the wall
  band between them, shown in the base state and hidden in this one.  Keep
  the two jambs at x13 and x18 in BOTH states - they are part of the wall,
  not the plug - so the opening keeps its reveal when the plaques are in
  place.  This is the warp to HALL_OF_HONOR at x14 -> x10,y22 and
  x16 -> x12,y22, so it must be walkable in the open state.

NOTES
  The notch at x14-17 in the raised field's north edge (y8) already lines up
  with this doorway; the carpet axis x14-17 from the south door to the Hall
  of Honor is the room's processional line and is clear in both states.
  The two plaques are the only place in the game where a door is disguised as
  decoration - keep them readable when closed.

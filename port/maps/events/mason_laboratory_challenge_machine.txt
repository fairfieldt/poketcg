EVENT - MASON LABORATORY, CHALLENGE MACHINE INSTALLED
=====================================================
Image: events/mason_laboratory_challenge_machine.png
This is NOT a separate room.  It is mason_laboratory.txt with one object
added; build the base room once and toggle this on it.

WHAT IS IDENTICAL
  Every wall, every floor tile, the workstation, the desk, all eleven orange
  specimen cabinets, the green duel table, the four stools, the palm,
  the floor, the lighting and the palette are unchanged, tile for tile.

WHAT CHANGES - exactly one 4 x 5-tile patch
  x10-13, y0-4 on the NORTH WALL, immediately west of the green wall display.
  In the base room this patch is plain wall - the gold-and-white checkerboard
  band and the olive panelling continue through it uninterrupted.  In this
  state a machine is installed there.

THE CHALLENGE MACHINE - x10-13, y0-4, 4 x 5 tiles, about 2 units wide
  Build it as four stacked parts, front face flat-on to the camera:
    y0-y1  SUPERSTRUCTURE.  A black #000000 open frame packed with gold
           #bd8c39 and white pipework: vertical columns, elbows, a T-junction
           and short white stubs.  Read it as exposed ducting and coolant
           pipes on top of a cabinet, not as decoration.
    y2     MONITORS.  Two identical pale-cyan #bfe8ff CRTs side by side, each
           with a black bezel, each showing a bright MAGENTA #ff00ff and
           pale-cyan CHECKERBOARD on a 2 x 2-pixel pitch that fills the tube.
           The magenta is the machine "running".
    y3     CONSOLE BODY.  A blue #3979ff carcass with two long white readout
           bars - a wide one on the left, a narrower one on the right - and a
           small dark port below.
    y4     CONTROL DESK.  A blue sloped panel with a grid of pale-cyan keys -
           four keys left, three right - on a blue base.
  It stands against the wall about 2 units tall and is impassable; the player
  interacts with it from x10-13, y5.

HOW TO SHIP IT
  Model the machine as a single prop with a boolean "installed" flag, and a
  second material variant on the two CRTs (magenta checker = active, plain
  dark blue = idle).  Nothing else in the room needs a variant.

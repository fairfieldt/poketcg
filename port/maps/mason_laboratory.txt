MASON LABORATORY - 3D room brief
================================
Image: mason_laboratory.png - 28 x 30 tiles = 224 x 240 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: MASON_LABORATORY.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  Dr.
Mason's card research lab - the game's first room.  Shares its tileset and
palette with deck_machine_room.txt; build that set once and reuse it.

CONNECTION
  South door x14-17 at y28 -> OVERWORLD_MAP.
  East door x26 at y10 and y12 -> DECK_MACHINE_ROOM at x2,y10 / x2,y12.
  Two exits; the rest is dead end.  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-25, y5-26 minus the machines and counters below.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y27-29.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27 (with the door).
  Wall build: dark olive-brown #4a3c00 panelling with a gold #b5a100 pinstripe
  and a white #ffffff inner outline.  The decorated row (y1, mirrored at y27)
  is a cream #ffeef0 band carrying a gold-and-white 8 px checkerboard, broken
  only by the door.  Height 4-5 units.  This is the Mason Laboratory /
  Deck Machine Room livery - do not invent another.

FLOOR
  Pale cyan #ccffff tiles with a medium blue #52a5ff grout on every tile edge,
  drawn as short blue bars so the grid reads as a bright institutional tile
  floor.  Flat, one texture, the whole room.

WORKSTATION - north-west corner
  x2-8, y1-4.  A computer workstation: a white cabinet with a pale-blue top at
  y1, a blue CRT monitor with a white highlight at y2, a blue control panel
  with a row of white keys at y3, and a brown base with a dark toe at y4.
  Immediately in front of it, x4-9, y2-4, is an orange-brown #b55a00 desk
  carrying a black articulated instrument arm (a lamp or microscope) that
  reaches up to y1 at x8-9.  Impassable, about 1 unit high.

WALL DISPLAY - north wall, centre
  x14-15, y1-3.  A small wall-mounted readout: a heavy black-framed box with
  a green #00b500 screen carrying a few white bars, set into the checker band.
  About 1.5 units tall, flat against the wall.  It is present in every state
  of this room.
  NOTE: the Challenge Machine itself is NOT built in the base room.  The wall
  at x10-13, y0-4 - immediately west of this display - is plain checkerboard
  here, and only becomes a machine in
  events/mason_laboratory_challenge_machine.png.  Leave that 4 x 5-tile patch
  as plain wall and model the machine as a toggleable prop.

STORE CABINETS - four, north wall east
  x17-19, x20-22, x23-25 and one more at x25-26, y1-4 - four identical tall
  cabinets 3 x 4 tiles: an orange #b55a00 carcass with a dark interior, three
  shelves holding small pale bottles and canisters, and a closed orange cupboard
  with a dark handle at the bottom.  About 2 units tall, impassable.
  Model one, instance four (and reuse the mesh in the south-east).

BENCH AND COUNTER - mid room
  * x20-26, y14-16: three more of the same cabinets, in a row on the east side.
  * x18-26, y26-27: a low counter/bench run against the south-east wall.

COLLECTION BOOTH - the centre of the room
  x8-12, y14-19.  The room's main prop: a large machine with a bright green
  #00b500 face set in a heavy black frame, the face carrying dark rectangular
  bays and small white indicator lamps, standing behind a brown #b57300 counter
  with a black-toothed front at y18-19.  About 2 units tall, impassable.  Give
  the counter a workable top so the player can stand at it.

BALLOONS - four
  Bright magenta #ff00ff balloons with a white highlight and a short dark
  string, 1 tile each, at x5,y14; x11,y14; x5,y17; x11,y17 - clustered round
  the west side of the collection booth.  They are decoration only; float them
  at about 1.8 units.

POTTED PALM - south-west corner
  x2-4, y22-26.  A fat brown #b55a31 pot with a thick trunk and a fan of
  green #00b500 / dark-green fronds, about 2 units tall.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_DRMASON x14,y6 at the head of the room under the Challenge Machine.
  NPC_TECH5 x6,y4 at the desk, NPC_TECH1 x22,y8 and NPC_TECH2 x22,y20 and
  NPC_TECH3 x22,y22 along the east cabinets, NPC_TECH4 x10,y22 in the south,
  NPC_SAM x4,y14 at the west wall.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Bright cyan-tiled floor, dark olive walls with gold and a gold checker,
  orange-brown cabinetry, one large green machine face, four magenta balloons.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE MASON LABORATORY
  The green-screened collection booth in the middle of an otherwise empty
  cyan-tiled hall, a small green wall readout under the north checker band,
  a computer workstation and instrument desk in the north-west corner,
  eleven orange specimen cabinets lining the east and north walls, four
  magenta balloons and a corner palm.

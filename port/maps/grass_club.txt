GRASS CLUB - 3D room brief
==========================
Image: grass_club.png - 28 x 32 tiles = 224 x 256 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: GRASS_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  The largest
and most "garden" room: an outdoor lawn inside a log fence, with topiaries,
flower beds and scattered stones.  Layout is a loose grid, not mirrored.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y30 -> GRASS_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.
  Only warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... the whole lawn x2-25, y2-27 except where a bed,
                        topiary, basket or stone stands.
  Perimeter ........... y0-1 and y28-31 and x0-1 and x26-27 are a LOG FENCE,
                        not a masonry wall: a row of vertical tan #f7c652
                        posts with dark #631000 grain lines and dark gaps,
                        capped along the top (y1, mirrored y28) by a dark
                        crimson #631000 horizontal rail, with a strip of
                        dark-green #006300 hedge outside it and a lime
                        #ffff84 outline.  The fence is about 2 units tall and
                        you see hedge over the top of it.  It is a fence, not
                        a wall - leave gaps at the post bottoms and let the
                        sky be bright.

FLOOR
  Bright lawn #21ef21 with a dark #006300 grid line every tile (it reads as
  mown turf in 1-tile squares), scattered with single light-green #ffff84
  tufts/specks and a few small clusters of them.  Completely flat.
  Scatter 50-80 small tuft decals at the same density as the PNG; do not
  tile a uniform grass texture - the speckling is the look.

FLOWER BEDS - six, all flush planters
  Each is a rectangular bed with a navy #00005a kerb, a bright blue #5263ff
  and cyan #52e7ff interior and a dense scatter of yellow #ffff00 blossom
  dots; the two long ones on the north side also have a row of solid blue
  #5263ff blocks along their bottom kerb.  They are flat or at most 0.2 units
  proud.
    1. x4-9,  y3-4    6 x 2   against the north fence, west
    2. x14-23 y3-4   10 x 2   against the north fence, east
    3. x24-25 y6-10   2 x 5   east fence, upper
    4. x2-3,  y8-12   2 x 5   west fence, upper
    5. x24-25 y14-18  2 x 5   east fence, lower
    6. x2-3,  y14-18  2 x 5   west fence, lower
  They are impassable.  Note beds 3 and 4 are NOT at the same height: the
  east one starts one row further north.

TOPIARIES - eighteen, one mesh, four rows
  Each is a small ornamental tree on a 2 x 3-tile footprint: a flared
  tan #f7c652 / crimson #631000 trunk about 0.5 units tall, carrying a round
  clipped green head (dark #006300 shell, mid #006300 and light #21ef21
  checker foliage) with a small green star-sprig on top.  Total height about
  1.5 units.  Impassable.
    Row 1, y6-8  : x14-15, x16-17, x18-19                      (3)
    Row 2, y10-12: x14-15, x16-17, x18-19                      (3)
    Row 3, y16-18: x6-7, x8-9, x10-11, x12-13, x18-19, x20-21 (6)
    Row 4, y20-22: x6-7, x8-9, x10-11, x12-13, x18-19, x20-21 (6)
  Rows 3 and 4 are aligned pairs with a 4-tile gap between the two blocks and
  a 4-tile aisle at x14-17.  Rows 1-2 form a 3 x 2 hedge square in the
  north-east quarter.

FLOWER BASKETS - eight, one mesh
  Round brown #c66b00 basket pots, 2 x 2 tiles, overfilled with pink
  #ffb5ff and white blossoms and a couple of green leaves.  About 0.6 units
  tall, impassable.
    A cluster of four in a 2 x 2 block at x8-9 / x10-11, y8-9 / y10-11
    (dead centre of the west half of the lawn).
    Four at the south corners: x2-3 y24-25, x24-25 y24-25,
                              x2-3 y26-27, x24-25 y26-27.

STONES - 57 clusters
  Small grey #9c9c9c / white #ffffff boulders with a dark outline, 1 tile or
  less across, about 0.3 units tall, dropped at random all over the lawn -
  47 single stones, 8 pairs, 1 triple at x9-10 y14-15 and one 5-stone clump
  at x22-23 y22-25.  They are impassable.  Do not place them on the paths
  between the topiary rows; they cluster in the open lawn.

SOUTH ENTRANCE RUNNER
  A red #ff1000 and white #ffffff patterned runner, 4 tiles wide (x12-15),
  from y28 to y31, laid across the fence line at the doorway: a red ground
  with a white diamond/lattice pattern and a white fringe.  It is the only
  red in the room.  Model it as a flat decal that crosses the fence line.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_NIKKI x12,y4 - the club opponent, at the north fence between beds 1 and
  2.  NPC_KRISTIN x4,y10 on the west lawn by bed 4.  NPC_HEATHER x14,y16 in
  the central aisle between the topiary rows.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  One saturated green field, a tan log fence, blue-and-yellow beds, clipped
  green topiaries, pink baskets, grey stones, and a single red-and-white
  runner at the door.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE GRASS CLUB
  A walled garden rather than a room: a log fence with hedge behind it, mown
  square turf, six blue flower beds set into the fence line, eighteen clipped
  topiaries in four rows, eight pink flower baskets, and 57 loose stones
  scattered over the lawn.

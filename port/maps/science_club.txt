SCIENCE CLUB - 3D room brief
============================
Image: science_club.png - 28 x 32 tiles = 224 x 256 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: SCIENCE_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  A laboratory
in violet: the whole shell is one purple, and every prop is a machine.
Roughly symmetric shell, asymmetric furniture.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y30 -> SCIENCE_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.
  Only warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-25, y5-27, minus the machines listed below.
  North wall band ..... y0.   South wall band ..... y28-31.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27.
  Wall build: flat violet #6b00ff with a bright white #ffffff outline along
  its inner edge, standing on a CYAN-AND-WHITE CHECKERBOARD baseboard
  (#52e7ff / #ffffff, 8 px squares) that runs the full width of the south
  wall at y29-30 and reappears under the north console bank.  Height 4-5
  units.  The white edge is thick (1 tile) - it is what makes the room read
  as a clean lab rather than a dark cave.

FLOOR
  Light lilac #e7b5ff tiles with a violet #6b00ff grout line on every tile
  edge and a slightly darker #ad6bff tile face, so the floor reads as a grid
  of 1-tile purple squares.  Flat, one texture, everywhere.

WORKBENCH - north-west corner
  x2-5, y1-4.  A 4 x 4-tile workbench with an orange #e76318 top and a dark
  #5a1008 base with a lighter toe rail.  On the top sit two dark instruments
  (a tilted telescope/microscope body and a small stand) and a white square at
  the near-left corner (a lamp or a terminal).  Impassable, about 1 unit
  high.  It is the only warm-orange object in the north half of the room.

COMPUTER BANKS - two, against the north wall
  Each is an 8-9 x 4-tile console run: x7-15, y1-4 and x18-25, y1-4.
  Construction: a white #ffffff carcass with a black outline, a cyan #52e7ff
  and white dotted worktop, and, standing on it, TWO CRT MONITORS each, so
  FOUR monitors in total across the room.  Each monitor is 2 x 2 tiles: a
  black-framed tube displaying a colour-bar test card (yellow #ffff00, green
  #00ff00, blue #0000ff, red #ff0000 bars) on a light-purple face, with a
  small control panel of cyan dots and a knob below it.  Impassable, about
  1.5 units high.  These four colour bars are the brightest thing in the
  room and are its signature.

STORE RACK - west wall
  x2-5, y13-16.  A 4 x 4-tile orange #e76318 shelving unit, built as two
  2 x 4 shelves side by side, each with three open compartments holding dark
  bottles, boxes and equipment.  About 1.5 units tall, impassable.

LAB BENCHES - two, back to back with the walkway
  A pair of long low benches on the middle-left of the room: x7-11, y12-13
  and x12-16, y12-13, each 5 x 2 tiles, butted together to form a single
  10 x 2 run.  Top: a cyan #52e7ff and white dotted surface; body: a green
  #009442 and white front panel with a dark base.  About 0.8 units high,
  impassable.  A black-and-white kerb line runs along y11 from x2 to x17 and
  back along y14 from x7 to x17, so the benches sit on a shallow outlined
  platform with the store rack at its west end.

BENCH MACHINE - centre of the room
  x12-15, y9-10.  A 4 x 2-tile instrument on a low stand: a light-purple
  carcass with a cyan dotted top and a four-colour panel (yellow / green /
  blue / red) at its centre.  Impassable, about 0.8 units high.  It sits
  directly on the room's axis, 4 tiles north of the benches.

TEST RANGE - four identical machines, south-east
  x14-25, y20-22 - four machines in a row, each 3 x 3 tiles at x14-16,
  x17-19, x20-22 and x23-25, y20-22, spaced 3 tiles apart with no gaps.
  Each is a white-outlined violet cabinet with a blue #0000ff square screen
  in its upper half, a maze of white piping traced across its face, two red
  #ff1000 caps on top and a dark base.  About 1.2 units tall, impassable.
  Model one, instance four.

SOUTH ENTRANCE RUNNER
  A red #ff1000 and white #ffffff patterned mat, 4 tiles wide (x12-15),
  y28-31, across the doorway: a red ground with a white diagonal lattice and
  a white border.  Flat decal.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_RICK x4,y4 at the workbench, NPC_DAVID x20,y4 under the east computer
  bank, NPC_JOSEPH x8,y10 on the west side of the central aisle, NPC_ERIK
  x6,y18 on the west lawn-side.  Keep the central aisle x10-15 clear from the
  door to the north wall - it is the only route through.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  One violet key light over everything, white outlines, cyan checker, and the
  only saturated colour coming from the four CRT test cards and the red door
  mat.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE SCIENCE CLUB
  A violet-tiled laboratory ringed with white-outlined purple walls on a
  cyan-checker baseboard: a workbench, two console banks with four colour-bar
  CRTs, an orange store rack, two dotted-top lab benches on a kerbed
  platform, and a row of four identical blue-screened test machines.

LIGHTNING CLUB LOBBY - 3D room brief
====================
Image: lightning_club_lobby.png - 28 x 26 tiles = 224 x 208 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: LIGHTNING_CLUB_LOBBY.
Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder for the coordinate/scale convention, then
build the room from club_lobby_3d.txt in this folder.

THIS ROOM IS IDENTICAL TO THE OTHER EIGHT CLUB LOBBIES
  All nine lobbies share TILEMAP_CLUB_LOBBY_CGB, TILESET_CLUB_LOBBY and
  PALETTE_CLUB_LOBBY; pairwise comparison gives 728/728 identical tiles.  The
  geometry, the art and the colours do not change at all between clubs - do
  NOT tint this room to the Lightning theme.  The only per-club difference is the
  NPC list below and the map the east door leads to.

WHAT IS IN THIS FILE
  Everything in club_lobby_3d.txt: the counter + Card Pop machine on the west
  side, the duel table with four red chairs in the south-west, three
  bookcases against the north-east wall, two framed pictures and a wall sign
  on the north wall, a pair of potted palms in the south corners, an entrance
  rug at the east door, and the shared club livery (brown/gold/white walls,
  pale-green gridded floor).

CONNECTION
  East door x26 at y10-13 -> LIGHTNING_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x2, y6 / y2, y8 (the club
  foyer).  That is the only warp: the lobby is a side room off the foyer and
  the club room itself is reached from the foyer, not from here.

PEOPLE IN THE ROOM (6 of them; positions from the map's NPC script, they
are not drawn in the PNG)
    x18, y2   NPC_IMAKUNI              against the north wall
    x6 , y4   NPC_CLERK10              behind the reception counter
    x10, y4   NPC_GIFT_CENTER_CLERK    behind the reception counter
    x20, y8   NPC_HOOD1                out on the floor
    x8 , y12  NPC_LASS4                out on the floor
    x18, y16  NPC_CHAP2                out on the floor
  Standing positions are given as scripted.  Two of them are behind the
  reception counter (the counter top is at y6-8, so y4 is behind it): the
  club clerk at x6 and the gift-centre clerk at x10 - the gift clerk is the
  one who hands you a promo card.  The rest stand out on the floor as
  challengeable members; several of them sit on the duel table's chairs.
  Keep at least one free chair at the table so the room still reads as a
  play area.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE LIGHTNING CLUB LOBBY
  Nothing you can see.  Same room, same colours, same props - only the people
  and the door destination differ.

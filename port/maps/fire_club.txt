FIRE CLUB - 3D room brief
=========================
Image: fire_club.png - 28 x 32 tiles = 224 x 256 px at 8 px/tile.
Game map: FIRE_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md in this folder first.  The simplest,
most formal room: a perfectly symmetric hall, a hot checkerboard floor, cold
blue walls, and a red carpet down the middle.  Everything on the left is
mirrored on the right.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y30 -> FIRE_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.
  Only warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north straight down the carpet.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-25, y3-27, entirely open except the six braziers.
                        There are no other obstacles: the room is one big
                        clear hall.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y28-31.
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27.
  Wall build: deep blue #0000e7 panels with a white #ffffff outline and a
  light-blue #42a5ff inner bevel.  The decorated rows (y1-2 north, y29-30
  south) carry the FIRE CLUB MOTIF: a run of concentric nested squares -
  white, then blue, then light blue, then a white centre - each unit 2 tiles
  wide and 2 tall, repeated 12 times across the width and interrupted only by
  the carpet at x12-15.  Use exactly the same motif as fire_club_entrance.txt
  and fire_club_lobby.txt - it is the club's livery.  Height 4-5 units.

FLOOR
  A checkerboard: alternating tiles of plain cream #ffffc6 and tiles carrying
  an orange #ff9c39 open cross-hatch, so the floor reads as a warm cream-and-
  ember chequered tile at 1-tile scale.  Flat, one texture, the whole room.
  EMBLEM INLAYS: small pink #ffd6ff four-petal rosettes, 1 tile each, set
  into the floor in two vertical columns at x=11 and x=20, at y=5, 9, 13, 17
  and 21 - five each, ten in total, spaced every 4 rows.  They are the only
  cool colour on the floor and they mark the two walkways either side of the
  carpet.

RED CARPET
  A runner exactly x12-15 (4 tiles wide) from y3 to y31 - from the north wall
  to and through the south doorway, the full length of the room.  Pattern: a
  red #ff0000 ground with a dark #840000 and white diagonal lattice forming
  elongated diamonds along its length, with dark selvedges at x11 and x16
  drawn as a thin dark strip with a light notch every other row.  Flat decal,
  walkable.  It is the room's axis and its only large warm mass.

BRAZIERS - six, one mesh, mirrored
  Each stands on a 2 x 5-tile footprint and is about 2 units tall:
    - y+0..1  the head: a red #ff0000 domed cap with a dark #840000 shadow
              band and a cream highlight, drawn as a wide brim over a narrower
              neck - a brazier hood or a mushroom cap;
    - y+2     a short cream #ffffc6 neck with orange detailing;
    - y+2..4  the pedestal: a green #84ff39 body with a dark-teal #005a31
              diagonal lattice (a woven or coiled wrap), tapering slightly,
              on a dark base.
  Positions (2 x 5 each):
      x6-7,  y4-8      x20-21, y4-8      (far pair)
      x6-7,  y12-16    x20-21, y12-16    (middle pair)
      x6-7,  y20-24    x20-21, y20-24    (near pair)
  All six are evenly spaced on a 8-row grid, 13 tiles apart horizontally,
  straddling the two rosette columns.  Impassable.  Model one, instance six,
  and put an orange point light in each hood.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_KEN x14,y4 - the club opponent, at the head of the carpet against the
  north wall.  NPC_JONATHAN x18,y10 and NPC_ADAM x8,y14 flank the middle of
  the hall between the braziers.  NPC_JOHN x12,y18 stands on the carpet in
  the near half.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Cold deep-blue walls with white-and-blue nested squares, a hot cream-and-
  ember checkerboard floor, one saturated red axis, six green-and-red
  braziers, ten tiny pink rosettes.  The wall/floor temperature contrast IS
  the room.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE FIRE CLUB
  The nested-square blue livery on every wall, a cream-and-ember chequered
  floor with ten pink rosette inlays in two columns, a red diamond-lattice
  carpet running the full length through the door, and six identical green-
  pedalled red-hooded braziers in a mirrored 2 x 3 grid.

PSYCHIC CLUB - 3D room brief
============================
Image: psychic_club.png - the tilemap is 28 x 32 but only y0-27 is drawn;
rows y28-31 of the PNG are black void.  Treat the room as 28 x 28 tiles =
224 x 224 px of usable space.  Game map: PSYCHIC_CLUB.  Read 3D_GUIDE.md
first.  Perfectly symmetric about the vertical axis; a long, narrow, shrine-
like hall.

CONNECTION
  South door x12-15 at y26 -> PSYCHIC_CLUB_ENTRANCE at x8,y2 / x10,y2.
  Only warp; dead end.  Fixed camera looking north straight down the carpet.

SHELL
  Walkable floor ...... x2-25, y3-24 plus the carpet strip; the eight columns
                        are the only blockers.
  North wall band ..... y0-2.   South wall band ..... y25-26 (nothing below).
  West wall band ...... x0-1.   East wall band ...... x26-27.
  Wall build: a bright BLUE ARCADE.  The wall bands (y1-2 and y25-26, and the
  side bands) are a repeating run of pointed arches drawn in blue #0000ff
  outlines with light-purple #9c6bff and white #ffffff fills on a dark blue
  ground, separated by narrow amber #f7ad00 and light-yellow #ffff9c vertical
  pilasters.  The arch motif is 2 tiles wide and repeats 12 times along the
  north and south bands.  The carpet passes under the arch in the middle of
  both the north and south bands (x12-15 at y3 and y26 are open arches).
  Height 4-5 units; the arcade is a flat decorated wall, not a real colonnade.

FLOOR
  Light yellow #ffff9c field with an amber #f7ad00 lattice: a grid of 2 x
  2-tile squares outlined in amber, with a darker amber cross at every
  intersection and a faint orange dot in the centre of each panel.  Flat, one
  texture, covers the whole room.  It reads as inlaid wood or a tatami-style
  parquet.

RED CARPET
  A runner exactly x12-15 (4 tiles wide) from y4 to y27 - the full length of
  the room, through the doorway.  Pattern: a red #ff0000 ground with a dark
  #5a0000 and white diagonal lattice (a repeating 4 x 4-tile diamond
  trellis), bounded on x11 and x16 by a 1-tile dark-red border strip with a
  light notch every other row.  Flat decal; walkable.  It is the room's axis.

ORNAMENTAL COLUMNS - eight, one mesh, the only 3D props
  The source shows a square dark-purple inset in a lavender/cream frame,
  above a fluted navy-and-cream shaft and a low plinth. It does not depict
  pointed flames. Preserve the broad framed square and architectural shaft;
  their precise fictional function is unspecified by the background art.
  Total height about 2.5 units. Impassable.
  Positions (source crop top-left, each 2 x 5 tiles):
      x10/y4, x16/y4, x6/y6, x20/y6,
      x6/y14, x20/y14, x10/y16, x16/y16.
  The eight form a mirrored octagon around the central carpet.

NPCS (scripted, not drawn in the PNG)
  NPC_MURRAY1 x12,y6 and NPC_MURRAY2 x2,y2 - the two-headed opponent, one on
  the carpet at the far end, one in the north-west corner.  NPC_DANIEL x8,y8
  and NPC_STEPHANIE x22,y12 stand between the columns.

COLOUR SCRIPT
  Yellow-amber parquet, a saturated red runner, blue-and-purple arcade walls,
  and eight framed purple ornaments.  Warm floor, cold walls.

WHAT MAKES THIS ROOM THE PSYCHIC CLUB
  A long arcade hall of blue-and-purple pointed arches around a yellow lattice
  floor, bisected end to end by a red diamond-lattice runner, with eight
  square-topped columns arranged in a mirrored octagon.

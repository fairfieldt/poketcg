All 124 world/UI scenarios passed: 62 at 320x240/4 MiB and the same 62 at 480x360/8 MiB. Each case warms 32 frames and samples 96 production frames; the same immutable diagnostic ROM is used for both runs. This full baseline predates the queue-wakeup backport; subsequent targeted cache comparisons use the fixed queue.

The main slowdown is repeated 2D canvas work. At 480, Sam's three-choice screen measures 18.45 game FPS (54.18 ms work), two-choice 19.48 FPS, and naming 16.40 FPS (60.98 ms work). The same Sam conversation camera without the panel remains near 30 FPS with about 23.5 ms work. The Mason panel adds about 11.1 ms at 320 and 23.5 ms at 480. This supports keeping model quality and first reducing repeated UI paint work; a focused CPU-paint versus RDP-compose split follows before the retained-canvas change.

Most 3D views sustain the 30 FPS cap in this emulator proxy at both resolutions. Challenge Hall lobby is about 25.8 FPS; Grass Club about 27.4. The overworld has a small 480 shortfall at 29.4 FPS. Interior work costs are almost identical between resolutions in this proxy, which points toward geometry/submission costs but does not measure physical-console RDP fill capacity.

The separate optimized duel/UI harness adds 18 measurements (nine per resolution, both 8 MiB, audio stubbed). Normal board idle, action and slot-cursor screens now sustain about 30 FPS. The remaining 480 common 2D paths are slower: hand/card list 8.41 FPS, card detail 10.74 FPS, generic popup 16.18 FPS. These should take priority over broad model cuts. Native before/after images are verified pixel-identical by that harness.

All timings come from the N64 CP0 clock within ares, with emulated RSP execution and host-backed RDP waits. They are not physical-console FPS. Full raw distributions, stage definitions, native logs, ROM hashes and limitations are in results.json and the linked native evidence. Work removes display acquisition/pacing wait; draw combines CPU/RSP/RDP; input polling and VM work outside the sampled callbacks are excluded.

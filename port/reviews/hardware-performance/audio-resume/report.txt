The tutorial reward crash is fixed.

The asset was valid. The original reward script calls pause_song, plays MUSIC_BOOSTER_PACK, waits for the fanfare, and resumes the prior music. The N64 shim closed and reopened the background WAV64 and then set its saved sample position. VADPCM is stateful and supports only encoded seek points. music09 has a loop seek point at sample 142656; the reported 652536 (0x9f4f8) is not a legal decoder seek. The original runtime reproduced the exact assertion in native ares.

The runtime now retains the paused WAV64, decoder state, and mixer samplebuffer on stereo pair 0/1. A zero playback rate holds its exact fractional position and zero master volume mutes both stereo channels. Temporary fanfares use pair 4/5. Resume closes the temporary stream and restores background frequency/volume without reopening or seeking. SFX remains on pair 2/3. Source wait-for-song uses the active music pair. Repeated/nested pause requests preserve the existing single-level source semantics; ended finite music stays ended.

The longer regression exposed a second independent failure: the SDK Huffman decoder estimates scratch space from the destination span. Short blocks at a music09 loop boundary can exceed that estimate. A project-local copy of the pinned libdragon decoder now uses separate aligned scratch with a proven worst-case bound: 846 decoded VADPCM bytes, at most two 8-bit symbols each, plus 8 bytes for unaligned/refill lookahead, rounded to 1712 bytes. It preserves RSP input/output and cache/fence ordering. EOF lookahead is zeroed, while actual consumed bits must remain inside bytes read. A deliberately truncated private asset is rejected by the new guard. The installed SDK and all shipped audio assets are unchanged.

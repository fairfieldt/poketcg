This experiment compares the unchanged current faces with three alternatives: B larger classic eye whites/pupils, C simplified dark eyes with glints, and D oversized cartoon eyes. All eleven facial styles are provided as opaque 32x32 textures with the original skin background. The N64 experiment samples six families: Mark, Dr. Mason, Nikki, Ronald, Ken and Granny.

The twelve images in `post-vi/` are original ares post-VI exports from an isolated DIAGNOSTIC=8 ROM. Portrait view uses the exact naming-screen 104x154 viewport and camera. Room view uses the production laboratory camera; conversation uses the tutorial conversation camera proportions. The experimental character lineups are fixed poses on clear floor anchors. Only face textures and review labels differ; geometry, cameras, skin backgrounds, lighting and VI settings are unchanged. `comparison-control.json` verifies no differences outside the facial regions and label.

Full exports are 640x240, displayed at 4:3. Enlarged face sheets crop the same areas across all options and apply nearest-neighbor pixel/aspect scaling only; `crop-metadata.json` records the crop rectangles. The independent `native/` frames are 320x240 RDP pixels before VI. The combined capture run passed on 4 MiB with 1,374,856 bytes of minimum free heap and exact callback-to-PNG equality for all twelve final video exports.

Production ROM, generated world/model assets and facial PNG pools are unchanged, verified by capture provenance. These are review options, not a production face change. Rebuild the isolated prototype with `make -C port/frontends/n64/adventure BUILD_DIR=build/face-preview ROM=poketcg-face-options DIAGNOSTIC=8 bundle`; the packer reads `port/characters/experiments/face-readability/manifest.json` and copies the existing native geometry byte-for-byte into option-scoped texture roots.

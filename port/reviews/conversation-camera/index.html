Conversation camera trials

A–D keep the same four actors, camera X/Z position, target and 50-degree field of view. Only camera height changes, so the distance also decreases as the camera lowers. E–F are separate closer two-person compositions with three-quarter poses.

My pick: 20 degrees with closer framing. The angle-only 20-degree view improves faces while keeping room depth. The closer views make Mark’s eyes, Ronald’s brow and smirk, and Mason’s glasses easier to read. The 12-degree view reveals more empty space above the back wall.

These are staged comparisons using the production room, models, renderer and dialogue panel. The production camera is unchanged. Ronald’s hair repair is included in both playable ROMs and the web build.

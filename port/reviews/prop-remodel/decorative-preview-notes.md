`decorative_props.py` geometry with the L643 vertex-colour emission material.
They are isolated shape reviews, not native N64 screenshots or VI output.

source collision footprints and competing coplanar faces both before and after

Original source/current inspection sheets are labeled by map name. Their right
columns use the checked-in Blender room previews, which predate this pass and
some camera/VI changes; they are not after screenshots.

Implemented

falloff, contact around physical prop bases, brighter aisles and focal areas,
and darker foreground framing. Textured box faces receive directional shade.
The completed character geometry, UVs, skin pigments, rig IDs and pivots remain

The island has ground-color contact/cast shading from building footprints and

Story/menu/diary panels and the island label share cream paper and blue ink.

Idle breathing is small and phased per person. Walking cadence remains tied

snapshot allocated only during transition and freed before destination load.
Duel shutters ease over 12 frames and hold closed for four frames. Browser
presentation uses the same assets and corresponding transitions.

Engineering decisions

An opaque ceiling slab would obscure these rooms from the elevated cameras;
taller graded walls and a warm background provide the framing instead.
Static contact is baked into floor colors rather than opaque black discs.
Dynamic human shadows use a small alpha fan supported by both renderers.
Libdragon GL already enables ordered square dithering; final VI dedither stays
enabled. RGBA16 remains the verified material format. Optional CI4 experiments
and further camera retuning are outside this pass.
